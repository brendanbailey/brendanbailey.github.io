Case Studies

Clustering with HDBSCAN

Lightning talk · General Assembly

Problem: DBSCAN is a go-to clustering algorithm, but it breaks down on clusters of varying density, splitting real groups and inventing spurious ones.

Approach: A lightning talk with a live notebook demo, built around a density-bar visualization, comparing DBSCAN and HDBSCAN on the same data and walking through how HDBSCAN works: core distance, mutual reachability, and the cluster hierarchy.

Result: HDBSCAN produced clean clusters where DBSCAN produced noise, with more control over how many clusters form, and it is faster with larger datasets.

Classifying Breast Tumors: Benign vs. Malignant

General Assembly · Python, scikit-learn

Problem: Given measurements from breast tumors, build a model that classifies each tumor as benign or malignant.

Approach: Compared two models head to head, logistic regression and random forest, tuning each with grid search and evaluating on held-out data with accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and ROC curves.

Result: Logistic regression hit 96% accuracy on the test set; the random forest hit 94% (98% on train, a sign of overfitting). I went with logistic: same ballpark, simpler, and its coefficients show which features push a tumor toward malignant or benign, which a forest can’t tell you.

LAPD Seeks Data-Driven Diversity

Talk · Predictive Analytics Innovation Summit, San Diego, Feb 2018

Problem: The LAPD’s racial demographics roughly matched the city’s makeup, but an aging force threatened that balance: retirements of Black officers were rising, not enough were coming through the pipeline, and 60% of Black officers were eligible to retire within five years.

Approach: Built a model to predict retirements from age, years in the LAPD, and gender, so staffing could see exactly when officers would leave and which candidates to target. With the LAPD equity work group, also traced where female candidates fall out of the process, from recruitment and training through resignation and termination.

Result: Staffing could plan hiring to keep the force representative of the city. The gender analysis found women passed the academy at 68% vs 87% for men in 2017, with 13% of women failing the weapons test and 8.5% resigning because they felt physically unprepared, which led to ideas including pre-academy weapons training, anxiety-relief classes, and prep materials.
